
How accurately can concrete compressive strength be predicted from mix composition and age?

* Best model: Gradient boosting (tuned)
    Test R²   = 0.942  (explains ~94% of the variance in strength)
    Test MAE  = 2.63 MPa
    Test RMSE = 4.14 MPa   (target range 2.3 to 82.6 MPa, mean ≈ 35.3 MPa)
* Linear baseline: R² = 0.580, RMSE = 11.19 MPa
    The nonlinear model cuts RMSE by 63%, which supports UCI's
    description of the relationship as highly nonlinear.
* 88% of test predictions are within ±5 MPa of the measured strength.
* Most important inputs (permutation): age, cement, water
* On completely unseen mix designs (grouped CV), R² = 0.882, RMSE = 5.50 MPa.
    This is the more honest estimate for a new mix.
